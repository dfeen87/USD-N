# USD-N Non-Goals (Scope Boundaries)

The USD-N codebase explicitly **does not** attempt to provide or imply:

- **Custody** of user assets or reserves.
- **Discretionary issuance** outside the deterministic policy action flow.
- **Promised convertibility** into USD, BTC, or any other asset.
- **Enforcement of acceptance** by merchants, platforms, or institutions.

Any change that contradicts these non-goals is out of scope and should be rejected.
